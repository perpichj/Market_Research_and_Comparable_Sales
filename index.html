Market Research & Comparable Sales

Investment Research Package · Preliminary valuation scenarios, comparable selection and market context

Autosaves in this browser. Export JSON for a portable backup. This tool generates preliminary research and scenario estimates, not an appraisal or certified valuation. Confirm brokerage/MLS permissions before commercial distribution of comparable information.

1. Subject property & assignment

Enter verified source information separately from assumed future renovation condition.

2. Comparable sales

Use recent, relevant sold properties. Adjustments are manual and must be supported by your market judgment; positive adjustments increase a comp's indicated value to reflect a subject advantage. Enter negative values when the comp is superior.

3. Market context & analysis

Enter sourced market metrics and explain any limitations in the available data.

4. Estimated value conclusion

Your working estimate can be weighted by comparable quality. These calculations are not a substitute for a supported valuation judgment.

5. Report disclosures

**Source discipline:** Verify facts against their original sources. Mark photos as owned/licensed before including them in a distributed report. Estimates, assumptions, and recommendations should not be described as verified facts. The report does not guarantee a sale price or future market conditions.
